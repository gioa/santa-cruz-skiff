# Santa Cruz wooden-skiff simulator research

Primary sources checked 2026-09-27. For game inspiration, not a current navigation / fishing guide.

## Confirmed anchors

- Santa Cruz Boat Rental operates from **15 Municipal Wharf, Santa Cruz**. Its rental page specifies a **16-foot skiff with 8 hp outboard**, PFDs for all passengers, full fuel tank, and safety / instructional briefing. https://santacruzboatrental.com/rentals/motorized-skiff-rentals-capitola/ (URL says Capitola but page title and address say Santa Cruz.)
- Sister operator Capitola Boat & Bait is on **Capitola Wharf, 1400 Wharf Rd**. Its about page explicitly identifies the fleet as **16-foot wooden skiffs with 8 hp outboards**. https://capitolaboatandbait.com/about-us/ . Capitola rental capacity is up to four: https://capitolaboatandbait.com/rentals/motorized-skiff-rentals-capitola/
- Official photograph directly inspected: **bright red painted hull**, **dark green gunwale and interior**, black hull number 21; long shallow open hull; raked, pointed bow and square transom; small gray Yamaha stern outboard; rods alongside and blue/yellow PFDs. This is one boat, not proof every boat has identical paint. Local reference: /private/tmp/skiff-reference.png . Source: https://santacruzboatrental.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/motorized-skiff-rental-capitola.png
- Operator lists rods/reels, saltwater line, hooks, sinkers, spoons and lures, and fresh/frozen sardines, anchovies, shrimp and squid bait. https://santacruzboatrental.com/capitola-fishing-gear/
- City 2023 environmental documents identify the private rental landing at row/bent 68, with a crane above it to lower wooden rental boats into the water. This supports the hoist, stairs and low boarding landing as historical/current-context scenery, but the exact operating procedure today was not verified. https://www.santacruzca.gov/files/assets/city/v/1/edh/edh-iam/documents/wharf/recirculated-partial-draft-eir-rpdeir/2-santa-cruz-wharf-master-plan-rpdeir-text.pdf
- The city describes the east side of the wharf as the **Santa Cruz Beach Boardwalk side**. The public landing is on that side across from the rental shop. https://www.santacruzca.gov/Government/City-Departments/Parks-Recreation/Santa-Cruz-Wharf
- City recreation information describes sea lions, otters and kelp beds around Cowell Cove. https://www.santacruzca.gov/Government/City-Departments/Parks-Recreation/Santa-Cruz-Wharf/Recreation
- The city 2023 document above describes sea lions resting on beams under the wharf, dolphins and otters, occasional whales, pelicans, cormorants, western gulls, and sardine / anchovy schools.
- NOAA describes rockfish and lingcod at rocky reefs, kelp greenling in kelp, sanddabs / sole at sandy bottoms; halibut and rockfish are resident sanctuary fish. This supports varied in-game habitats, not current harvest legality. https://montereybay.noaa.gov/media/reports/2006/fieldguide/2006fieldguide.pdf (p. 5)
- NOAA describes shallow rocky kelp forests and otters using kelp to reduce drift: https://montereybay.noaa.gov/science/characterization/kelp-forests.html

## Fictionalized design recommendations

These are original gameplay choices, not exact operator procedures or measured specifications.

1. A continuous walk along timber planks with white rails, many piles, a bait counter and small boat hoist; see the red skiff below before descending and boarding.
2. Physically collect PFD, rod, tackle, bait, cooler and landing net; place them visibly aboard. Keep cooler amidships, fuel aft, rod beside gunwale.
3. Model a 4.88 m open wooden hull, red outside, green inside, subtle chips revealing wood, three thin bench thwarts. Suggested width 1.55–1.75 m and bench count are artistic estimates. Avoid a varnished brown canoe or blocklike hull.
4. Board at a low platform and sit near the tiller. Keep actual player movement and boating transition spatially continuous. Separate neutral / forward / reverse from steering, retain momentum, use gentle roll and pitching, progressive wake and engine vibration.
5. Use gray-blue / muted green Pacific water, small chop, marine haze, shore hills and gulls. Wharf remains the return landmark; Boardwalk is east/landward, west coast cliff / lighthouse silhouette on other side.
6. Fish by choosing bait, casting / lowering visible line, waiting through subtle rod-tip taps, setting hook, reeling against tension, then showing fish near surface and landing / releasing. Reef, sandy and kelp-edge locations can produce different fictional encounter probabilities.
7. Return by navigating to the same dock, slowing / neutral, stopping alongside, tying up, unloading gear, and stepping back onto the wharf. Record trip time, distance and species.
