## Changes discovered during audit

- Removed nonfunctional drinking/sunscreen/headlamp bundle and communications/emergency bundle from pixel inventory and carried weight.
- Removed the obsolete life-jacket inventory toggle after swimming had been retired. Character equipment styling remains; no new drowning system.
- Existing anchor, drogue and loose reel/line/leader/sinker shop removals remain in place. Legacy ownership is preserved; removed items do not consume visible slots or item counts.
- Added direct backpack actions for chart and compass, alongside GPS and sounder.
- Connected rod sensitivity to low-load visible tip bend. It does not manufacture force, alter fish spawn probabilities or increase measured line load.
- Corrected float rig description: current premade rig uses its preset depth; it no longer advertises an adjustment UI that does not exist.
