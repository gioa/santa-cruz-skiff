# Wharf correction references · 2026-09-27

The user identified that the 06:00 rendering was too dark and the rental stair should be on the left when walking seaward. The former right-side floating pontoon was an invented arrangement and has been removed.

## Observed references

- [CDFW Santa Cruz survey site notes, 2008](https://filelib.wildlife.ca.gov/FileLib/CRFS/CRFS%20Training%20Materials/Sites/MON_SCR%20specific%20ADDENDA/MON_SCR_Addenda%202008.pdf): rental shop on the right when entering the wharf, directly across from the boat hoist.
- [City engineering report, 2014](https://www.santacruzca.gov/files/assets/city/v/1/edh/edh-iam/documents/wharf/reference-documents/santa-cruz-wharf-engineering-report-part-1.pdf): Photo 8-2 on PDF page 95 shows the small fixed rental landing at Bent 68, below the hoist; Photo 8-3 shows the separate public float. Page 16 gives a nominal 23 ft MLLW main wharf elevation; page 94 describes fixed landings at 8 ft MLLW. Timber piling, asphalt surfacing, white railings and parallel stairs inform the model.
- [Bay Area Waters boarding video, 2021](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFTl92aMnD8): actually inspected at 00:35 (green hoist and four-point sling), 00:45 (straight white-railed descent), 00:50–00:55 (small fixed weathered timber landing and paired round metal ladder handrails), and 01:10 (aboard the skiff).
- [Operator storefront photograph](https://assets-global.website-files.com/653a8d9440fb544e3dd25c12/65dc66d15c3628dbd8b07b39_santa-cruz-boat-rentals-7-p-800.jpeg), linked by [Capitola Boat and Bait](https://www.capitolaboatandbait.com/): turquoise frontage, two tiers of tall merchandising windows, shallow asymmetric roof, pale fascia and exposed rafter ends, narrow cream BOAT RENTALS sign, curb and angled parking bays.

Photographs/video were used for visual reference only and are not redistributed as game textures. Geometry and storefront sign artwork are authored. OSM still supplies the coastline and main wharf footprint. Stair/landing plan dimensions, interior stock, roof measurements and the approximate 1.45 m MLLW-to-rendered-water offset are estimates, not a survey. The historic references do not establish the exact condition of every railing in 2026.

## Dawn presentation

06:00 and 1:1 time progression are retained. Solar direction and direct sunlight remain astronomical. The monitor rendering now includes a bounded twilight exposure adaptation and blue diffuse/sky fill so the deck, staircase and water remain visible. This is a visibility-oriented eye/camera approximation, not a calibrated measurement of local dawn illuminance. Midday and deep night do not receive the exposure boost.
