# All equipment loss and hospitalization — v65

Replaces v64's retention of major equipment. Every owned item, including stowed gear, rods/assemblies, spare rigs, all bait, electronics and the PFD is lost on capsize. Catch-history/credits remain; unsettled catches retain v64's loss handling. Persistent equipmentLost marker prevents legacy createProfile starter grants on reload. packStarter only activates owned gear and cannot replenish equipment.

PFD is a usable bag item again. New games start wearing one. Old implicit-PFD saves migrate once to worn, but explicitly removed/missing PFDs stay off across unrelated equipment changes and reload. The pre-capsize worn state is captured before inventory loss; mere ownership is insufficient. A protected capsize costs 50 rescue credits. Without worn PFD, additional fictional hospital fee = 250 credits, with 8 extra active seconds of hospital/resuscitation-observation presentation before the harbor/night/next-day cycle. This is a game consequence, not a medical model or a real cost estimate. Unpaid rescue and hospital fees remain separately recorded; later earnings pay debts. Interrupted recovery does not apply losses/fees again.

Former free starter hardware remains free at character creation but has replacement shop prices: PFD 20, basic rod/reel 35, cooler 25, net 15, descending device 10 credits. Bait/rig purchases after loss restore ownership as well as consumable stock. No free post-capsize replenishment.
