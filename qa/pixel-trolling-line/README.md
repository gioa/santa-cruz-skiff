# v46 trolling line presentation

Diagnosis: the suspended rig already has independent water drag/inertia and trails a moving boat. The lowered line's world-space surface displacement was drawn at map scale (6 px/m), while the rod's visible height used enlarged artwork. This flattened its apparent angle and made it look fixed beside the boat. At 1 m/s with 15 m of braked line and an 85 g rig, after 20 seconds of towing the model's hook was about 8.8 m aft of the tip, but the visible entry moved only 9.4 px; it now moves about 22 px, preserving the physical horizontal/vertical ratio on the rod artwork.

Lowered lines now use the same height scale for both horizontal axes and vertical rod height. Cast lines intersect the visible rod-to-world-hook ray and preserve their exact surface target at splashdown. Underwater hooks remain invisible; only a short fading tail is drawn. This is a presentation correction: no changes to actual hook position, water-current physics, boat size, paid line, bite location, or tackle depth. Steady straight towing can legitimately settle at a stable relative angle; accelerating, turning and stopping change it.

Validation: 599 tests passed. Added acceleration/turn/stop, frame-rate consistency, uniform co-drift invariance, conserved paid line, two-axis display slope at multiple zooms, render immutability, and cast splash continuity checks. Browser rendered all four stages without console errors. `line-states.png` shows the production renderer on locally simulated states; the QA page and captions are excluded from deployment (only dist is hosted).
